Kanto Region Map

Key Locations

A: Pallet Town

A small town located in southwestern Kanto. The Oak laboratory, where the famous Professor Oak conducts his research, is located in the town. It is also said to be the hometown of a silent champion and the ambitious grandson of the professor himself.

B: Viridian City

Famously known as "The Eternally Green Paradise", Viridian City is enveloped by vibrant greenery all year round, containing majestic trees and vibrant flower beds. The city's gym, however, has been closed for years, and no one knows where the gym leader went following their abrupt departure.

C: Viridian Forest

Lying between Viridian City and Pewter City, many trainers get lost in this deep, sprawling forest containing long stretches of tall grass and thick, tall trees. Because this natural maze is full of Bug-type Pokémon, it is very popular among bug catchers and trainers beginning their journey.

D: Pewter City

Known as the "Stone Gray City", Pewter City is nestled between mountains and rocks—the preferred location for the city's local gym leader. The Pewter City Museum of Science can also be found here, featuring several scientific artifacts.

E: Victory Road

Situated on Route 23, Victory Road is a multi-floored cave that must be traversed by all trainers aiming for the top: the final challenge before arriving at the Indigo Plateau. Legend tells of a legendary bird engulfed in flames residing in the deepest recesses of the tunnels.

F: Indigo Plateau

Stationed on a mountain looking over the region, the Indigo Plateau serves as the capital of the Pokémon League in Kanto. Here, trainers face the ultimate test, pushing their skills to the absolute limit as they battle the Elite Four for the right to challenge the reigning Pokémon Champion.

G: Mt. Moon

Just east of Pewter City and west of Cerulean, Mt. Moon is a vast mountain range known for its frequent meteor showers, which are a sight to behold. The shards of fallen stars that can be found in its multi-floored cave system are called "star pieces" or "comet shards".

H: Cerulean City

Located in northern Kanto, Cerulean City is situated near a sea inlet to the northern ocean. The city is famous for its shimmering Nugget Bridge, and the streets and buildings feature vibrant blue tiling, reflecting the local gym leader's passion for Water-type Pokémon.

I: Saffron City

A sprawling golden metropolis of commerce, Saffron City is the largest city in Kanto and the tenth most populous city in the Pokémon world. The Silph Co. headquarters building stands firm as the city's centerpiece along with the gym, where multiple people have reported feelings of sudden disorientation the closer they get.

J: Celadon City

Known as "The City of Rainbow Dreams" when the city lights up at night, Celadon City is the most populous city in Kanto and the eighth most populous city in the Pokémon world, surpassing even Saffron City in the east. Its residents enjoy the pleasant aroma of flowers that radiate from the gym and spread across the city.

K: Rock Tunnel

Located east of Cerulean, Rock Tunnel is a naturally formed underground cave said to have formed after an earthquake many years ago. Since it has not been developed, it is pitch black inside. Trainers are advised to enter with a light source in order to avoid potentially aggressive Pokémon.

L: Power Plant

A power station that was abandoned many years ago after multiple incidents of faulty machinery hospitalized many workers. Since then, the building has been populated by Electric-type Pokémon absorbing energy from the few machines still working. Rumor has it the accidents were due to a mysterious bird that descended from the heavens after a furious thunderstorm.

M: Lavender Town

Located on the far east of Kanto, Lavender Town is bathed in an enchanting hue of purple that puts citizens at ease as they visit their departed Pokémon. While this calming hue brings peace to locals, a sudden cold chill seizes travelers the moment they step foot into town, with numerous reports of ghost sightings.

N: Vermillion City

Situated near a sea inlet to the south, Vermillion City serves as a popular seaport for trade ships—most famously the S.S. Anne, which regularly brings in thousands of visitors daily. The fresh smell of sea salt is overtaken near the gym, where constant flashes of blinding light can be seen during intense battles.

O: Fuchsia City

Located in southwest Kanto, many people gather in Fuchsia City to admire traditional buildings, visit the famous Safari Zone, and relax on the beach near Route 19. While the city is decorated with beds of beautiful pink flowers, visitors are highly advised not to touch them as they are highly poisonous.

P: Safari Zone

Located in Fuchsia City, the Safari Zone is a wide-spanning amusement park that allows visitors to explore and catch all sorts of Pokémon, including rare species such as Dratini. What makes this location unique is that trainers are not allowed to battle any of the Pokémon, needing to use bait and specially marked Safari Balls instead.

Q: Seafoam Islands

The Seafoam Islands are a pair of identical islands located between Route 20 and Route 19 in the southern ocean of Kanto. These islands contain deep cave systems that get colder the deeper you go. Many believe this is due to a fabled azure bird whose power to control ice has plunged the islands into a permanent cold spell.

R: Cinnabar Island

Located off the southern coast, Cinnabar Island is home to a large volcano that has long been dormant. Visitors travel up the volcano for a breathtaking view of the island, where those who reach the top can hear the ecstatic voice of the local gym leader who hosts an intense, fiery quiz every Thursday at 5:00 PM sharp.

S: Pokémon Mansion

Not much is known about Pokémon Mansion other than the fact it was the home of a famous researcher who conducted genetic experiments underground. However, the estate was recently found decrepit and burned down after a tragic accident, leaving few clues except for an empty containment pod.

T: Cerulean Cave

Also known as the "Unknown Dungeon", Cerulean Cave is a large underground cave system made up of complex maze-like tunnels that extend to underground lakes and deep pits. Unlike other cave systems, Cerulean Cave features sparkling silver sand and bright blue crystals decorating the walls and ceiling.

U: Diglett's Cave

Famously known for being created entirely by Diglett, Diglett's Cave is the only tunnel system in Kanto created entirely by Pokémon. It offers a convenient method of travel connecting Pewter City to Vermillion City, saving time for people seeking a ride aboard the S.S. Anne.

V: Pokémon Tower

Located in Lavender Town, Pokémon Tower is a seven-story building that houses the graves of countless Pokémon, with many people visiting daily to pay respects to their fallen partners. The tower is heavily populated by Ghost-type Pokémon who use illusions to cause extreme paranoia in visitors.

W: Pokémon Center

Scattered across the region and mainly found in cities and towns, Pokémon Centers are universal healthcare facilities and social hubs designed for Pokémon trainers and their partners. Highly popular for their friendly and attentive staff, Pokémon Centers provide a safe place for rest, medical support, and communication completely free of charge.

Routes

Route 1

Heading north from Pallet Town to Viridian City, many trainers begin their journey on Route 1. The area is filled with patches of tall grass and ledges that contour from the far east to the far west.

Route 2

Exiting Viridian City and heading north, Route 2 features a branching western and eastern path that offers trainers an entrance into Viridian Forest and an access point to Diglett's Cave.

Route 3

Just east of Pewter City, Route 3 is the eastern path towards Mt. Moon and Route 4, featuring an uneven zigzag layout filled with numerous branching ledges. Due to its large size, many trainers explore this route looking for battles or dropped items.

Route 4

Route 4 is split into two sections by Mt. Moon. The western portion makes up the entirety of Mt. Moon while the eastern section runs from the exit down towards Cerulean City. Small patches of tall grass are found on this path with wild Pokémon hiding amongst the greenery.

Route 5

Connecting Cerulean City in the north to Saffron City in the south, Route 5 is split into two pathways by ledges and the Saffron City gate. The central path features the Pokémon Daycare while the eastern path runs along a fenced line down toward the Underground Path entrance.

Route 6

Located in central Kanto running south of Saffron City, Route 6 is a winding S-shaped path connecting the Underground Path to Vermillion City. It features long patches of tall grass and a small pond.

Route 7

Route 7 is a very small path connecting Celadon City in the west to Saffron City in the east. Despite containing a field of tall grass, Pokémon rarely stay in the area for long. It also contains an Underground Path leading toward Route 8 and Lavender Town.

Route 8

Connecting Saffron City in the west to Lavender Town in the east, Route 8 features a winding S-shaped dirt road and a fenced-in garden patch. Due to its wide open space, many trainers visit the area to battle and catch rare Pokémon.

Route 9

Featuring a maze-like dirt path with small rock formations, Route 9 is popular among children for its uneven road and ledges that make it a great area for exploration. It connects Cerulean City in the west to Rock Tunnel and Route 10 in the east.

Route 10

Located southeast of Route 9, Route 10 offers a variety of paths. The route runs along a canal that features Rock Tunnel, and traveling along the water leads to the Power Plant. Continuing south leads trainers directly to Lavender Town.

Route 11

Traveling east from Vermillion City, Route 11 leads trainers to Route 12, located on the far east side of the region near the ocean. Along with containing the southern entrance of Diglett's Cave, Route 11 has a wide open field where many trainers challenge travelers to hone their skills.

Route 12

Just east of Vermillion City and running down south of Lavender Town, Route 12 features a long wooden path also known as the "Silent Bridge", as fishermen tiptoe along the bridge to avoid scaring fish. Rumors state a sleeping giant often blocks the upper path.

Route 13

Just west of Route 12's south end, Route 13 features a tight fence maze with coastal water views and colorful flower patches. Multiple sightings of a duck Pokémon holding a leek have been reported in the area, though many believe a Pokémon like that is far-fetched.

Route 14

A small, coastal route located in southeastern Kanto, Route 14 connects Route 13 on its north end down to Route 15 on its south end as trainers make their way to Fuchsia City. The route is known for its lush bushes that produce sweet and exotic berries.

Route 15

Connecting the south end of Route 14 in the east to Fuchsia City in the west, Route 15 is split by a raised long ledge section while the lower path is filled with small grass patches. It is a popular spot among Bird Keepers as the open space allows them to train Flying-type Pokémon.

Route 16

Located west of Celadon City, Route 16 is a small dirt path that leads to Route 17, offering a beautiful view of the ocean at the end of the road. Many bikers travel along this route in order to get to Cycling Road, filling the air with competitive spirit.

Route 17

More famously known as "Cycling Road", Route 17 is a long downhill slope running between Celadon City and Fuchsia City permitted only for cyclists due to the steep incline of the road. Fast-running Pokémon such as Dodrio and Rapidash are often seen racing cyclists.

Route 18

Route 18 is a small path connecting the southern end of Cycling Road to Fuchsia City on the east. It features a grassy field popular for its quiet, peaceful atmosphere, making it a popular rest spot for travelers.

Route 19

A popular spot for swimming and sunbathing, Route 19 is a water route located in southern Kanto connecting Fuchsia City in the north to Route 20 in the south. Despite being at sea, many trainers still battle on the ocean using Water-type Pokémon.

Route 20

Further off the mainland, Route 20 connects Route 19 in the east to Cinnabar Island in the west. Featuring the famous Seafoam Islands, Route 20 features strong currents and intense waves, making it a dangerous area for any traveler.

Route 21

Located south of Pallet Town, Route 21 serves as a vital ocean passage to Cinnabar Island. Aside from Route 20, it is one of only two pathways that lead trainers directly to the island's shores. Smaller islands can also be found along the route where battles take place.

Route 22

Exiting Viridian City, trainers make their final preparations as they prepare to enter the Pokémon League Gate just west of Viridian. Beyond this gate, the final path to the Elite Four opens, with the title of Pokémon Champion even closer in sight.

Route 23

Heading north from the Pokémon League Gate, Route 23 is a long vertical path featuring a central waterway where trainers must show League officials they have acquired all eight gym badges. At the path's end, the labyrinth of Victory Road awaits.
