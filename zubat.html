ZUBAT

♂ ♀
BAT POKÉMON
POISON
FLYING
HEIGHT 0.8 m
WEIGHT 7.5 kg

Making its home in dark caves, Zubat hang atop cave ceilings in large colonies sleeping throughout the day and hunting at night. Suffering from blindness, it emits ultrasonic waves from its mouth, using the returning echoes as a sonar to locate prey and navigate its surroundings.
